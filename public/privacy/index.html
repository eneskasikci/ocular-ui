Privacy Policy for Ocular

Effective date: November 19, 2026

Summary · Zero Data Collected

No personal data collected. No accounts. No tracking. Your photos, RAW captures, EXIF notes, and custom film recipes stay strictly on your device.

1. Data Collection

Ocular does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share any personal information, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or advertising identifiers.

Ocular v1 has no user accounts, no login system, and no backend servers operated by the developer.

2. Camera, Photos & Local Storage

All image processing happens locally on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Camera & Captures: Live viewfinder frames and captured JPEG, HEIF, or RAW/DNG photos are processed entirely on your device and saved to your Apple Photos library. Ocular never uploads or accesses your photos off-device.
  • Recipes & Preferences: Your custom film recipes and camera preferences are stored locally in app storage (UserDefaults) on your device.
  • Location & EXIF Metadata: Ocular records optical camera settings and optional film-look notes locally inside photo metadata. GPS location metadata is never written to photos unless you explicitly enable location tagging.

3. System Permissions & Revoking Consent

Ocular only requests iOS permissions directly needed for photography:

  • Camera: Used solely to display the live viewfinder and capture photos.
  • Photo Library: Used to save your captured photos, display your recent shots, and allow re-developing RAW photos with film recipes inside the darkroom.

You can review, limit, or revoke Camera or Photo Library access at any time in iOS Settings → Ocular. Paid features never depend on granting tracking or unrelated permissions.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Optional Ocular Pro subscriptions (monthly and annual plans, including any eligible 3-day introductory free trial) and the Lifetime purchase are processed exclusively by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store.

Ocular never receives, processes, or stores your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. To understand how Apple handles transaction data, please review Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party SDKs & Tracking

Ocular contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no tracking domains (NSPrivacyTracking is disabled in the app's privacy manifest). No third party receives user data through Ocular.

This website (ocularcamera.com) is purely static and sets no cookies, runs no analytics scripts, and loads no third-party tracking assets.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because Ocular stores no personal data on external servers, you remain in full control of all retention and deletion directly on your device:

  • Delete individual photos anytime in the Apple Photos app.
  • Reset camera preferences inside Ocular's Settings screen.
  • Uninstall Ocular from your device to permanently delete all locally stored custom recipes and app preferences.

7. Children's Privacy

Ocular does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes to This Policy

If Ocular's functionality changes in a future update (for example, if optional cloud sync is ever introduced), this Privacy Policy will be updated at https://ocularcamera.com/privacy with a new effective date before those changes take effect.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Ocular's privacy practices, contact the developer at ocular.camera@icloud.com. If you choose to email feedback from within the app, your mail app only sends the message and basic app, iOS, and device version details you choose to include, used solely to respond and improve Ocular.
