Frequently Asked Questions

How do custom film recipes work in Ocular Camera?

Ocular includes 12 analog film looks identified by standard 2–3 letter rangefinder HUD codes (STD, CC, AC, CN, NN, RA, ET, VV, AS, PNH, PNS, SEP). In the recipe editor, you can pair any base look with custom Kelvin white balance, Red/Blue matrix shift, highlight and shadow tone curves, dynamic range modes (DR100, DR200, DR400), 35mm film grain, and optical halation, and save them to custom slots C1–C7 to preview live in the viewfinder before pressing the shutter.

Can I shoot RAW or Apple ProRAW and re-develop with a different film recipe later?

Yes. Ocular Pro captures Bayer RAW (DNG) and Apple ProRAW while saving your active film recipe in EXIF metadata. Open any RAW capture in Ocular's built-in darkroom to scrub a live split comparison between the flat sensor RAW and your film look, or re-develop the photo non-destructively with any other recipe.

How do I restore my Ocular Pro purchase or Lifetime unlock?

Open Ocular and go to Settings → Restore Purchases (or tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the Ocular Pro paywall). Make sure your device is signed into the same Apple Account used for the original monthly, annual, or Lifetime purchase.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription or 3-day free trial?

Subscriptions and free trials are managed by Apple. You can tap Manage Subscription inside Ocular's Settings screen, or open iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Ocular to switch plans or cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

What happens to my custom recipes if Ocular Pro ends?

Your saved recipes are never deleted. Custom slot C1 stays active on the Free tier, and slots C2–C7 remain safely preserved on your device so they unlock immediately if you re-subscribe.

How do I update Camera or Photo Library permissions?

Open iOS Settings → Ocular to enable Camera or Photo Library access. Full Photo Library access allows Ocular to save shots, show recent captures, and re-develop RAW photos in the darkroom.

Where are my photos stored, and do I need an account?

Ocular does not use user accounts. Captured JPEG, HEIF, and RAW/DNG photos are saved directly to your Apple Photos library on your device.

Where can I read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use?
